中文English

簡單說

本 App 不需要註冊帳號，不收集你的姓名、電話、電郵或健康資料，不放廣告，也不追蹤你。

位置

App 會在你同意後讀取手機的大約位置，用來計算當地的日出、日落時間與時辰。座標會傳送給以下兩個公開服務，以取得日出日落時間與當地氣溫：

sunrise-sunset.org日出、日落時間
open-meteo.com當地氣溫與時區

傳送的只有經緯度，不含任何能辨識你身分的資料。這兩個服務的資料處理方式請參考它們各自的隱私權政策。

你可以拒絕定位。App 仍可使用，只是會改用預設地點計算時間。

存在手機上的資料

為了下次開啟更快，App 會在你的手機本機保存：最後一次的位置、日出日落資料、語言設定、夏令／冬令時間設定。這些資料只存在你的手機上，不會上傳到我們的伺服器（我們沒有伺服器）。刪除 App 即會一併刪除。

兒童

本 App 不會刻意收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下兒童。

健康聲明

App 內容為中醫養生知識分享，不能取代醫師的診斷與治療。

政策變更與聯絡方式

如本政策有變更，會更新此頁面與生效日期。如有疑問，請聯絡：施德享醫師，電郵 vauitsze@gmail.com。

Chrono Wellness Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short

The app requires no account. It does not collect your name, phone number, email or health information, shows no ads, and does not track you.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your approximate location to calculate local sunrise, sunset and the traditional two-hour periods. The coordinates are sent to two public services:

sunrise-sunset.orgSunrise and sunset times
open-meteo.comLocal temperature and time zone

Only latitude and longitude are sent, with nothing that identifies you. Please refer to those services' own privacy policies.

You may decline location access. The app still works, using a default location.

Data stored on your device

To load faster next time, the app saves on your device: your last location, sunrise and sunset data, language setting and daylight-saving setting. This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to us (we run no servers). Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Health notice

Content is for general wellness education based on Traditional Chinese Medicine and is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.
